Perspective

How I work with AI

AI helps me accelerate research, planning, implementation support, code review, testing, and documentation. It helps accelerate Angular and TypeScript modernization work, identify edge cases, draft coverage, and improve consistency.

Human ownership stays central for final architectural tradeoffs, security-sensitive decisions, and high-context product or UX decisions.

The goal is to use AI as a structured engineering assistant while keeping accountability and judgment human-owned.
